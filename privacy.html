Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

The short version: Hexle has no accounts, no ads, and doesn't collect anything that identifies you personally. Your game progress lives only in your own browser. The only things that leave your device are anonymous, aggregate usage numbers.

Who runs this

Hexle (hexle.us) is an independent, hobby-run game. There's no company behind it — just one developer. Questions or concerns can be raised via GitHub Issues.

Accounts and personal information

Hexle does not have user accounts, logins, or sign-ups. The game never asks for your name, email address, or any other personal information, and none is collected.

Game progress and settings (local storage)

Your stats, streak, and daily progress are saved using your browser's localStorage — a standard web technology that keeps data only on your own device. This data:

  • Never leaves your browser or gets sent to any server
  • Is not shared across devices (playing on your phone and your laptop keeps two separate histories)
  • Can be cleared at any time through your browser's own site-data settings, or by using the "Hard Reset" option in the game's Settings menu

Anonymous daily statistics

To power the "beats X% of yesterday's players" feature, Hexle sends a small anonymous signal to its own backend when you finish the official daily puzzle: just the date, the difficulty mode, and your result (e.g. "solved in 3" or "lost"). This is not linked to your identity in any way — no name, account, IP address, or device identifier is stored alongside it. It's only ever used in aggregate, to calculate what percentage of players did better or worse than a given result on a given day.

Analytics

Hexle uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand overall traffic (e.g. how many people visited, roughly which country from, which page loaded). Cloudflare Web Analytics is a privacy-focused product that does not use cookies or any persistent identifier, and does not track individuals across different websites. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy for details on how it handles data.

Cookies and tracking

Hexle itself does not set cookies and does not use any technology intended to track you across other websites, apps, or services. The only persistent browser storage it uses is localStorage, described above, which stays on your device.

Advertising

Hexle does not display any third-party advertising and is not part of any ad network.

Optional external links

The game includes an optional link to a Stripe-hosted page if you'd like to leave a tip for the developer, and a link to GitHub Issues for feedback. Both are entirely optional, open in a new tab, and are operated by those third parties under their own privacy policies — Hexle does not receive or see any information you provide on those external pages.

Children's privacy

Hexle is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If Hexle's data practices ever change, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will reflect it.
